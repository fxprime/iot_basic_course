# Getting Started with Tutoriaz

To get the most out of Tutoriaz courses, complete the following setup before the first session:

1. **Account preparation**
   - Confirm your username and reset your password if necessary.
   - Update your display name so instructors can identify you quickly in the dashboard.
2. **Hardware check**
   - Verify your ESP32 kit powers on and enumerate the serial port on your laptop.
   - Gather breadboard, jumper wires, LEDs, and sensors listed in the course syllabus.
3. **Software tooling**
   - Install the ESP-IDF or Arduino toolchain as your instructor prefers.
   - Log into the Tutoriaz student portal and remain on the lobby page for attendance tracking.

Once these steps are complete, you can follow along with the module materials hosted in this
documentation site.
